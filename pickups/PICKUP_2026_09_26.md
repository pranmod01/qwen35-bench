### Deep context — associative inference (unfinished, highest priority)

The task is ported and runs, but **it does not measure memory**. Across all three models and every
size from 2 to 500 chains, accuracy was 0.43–0.64 with the model answering the same side on ~100% of
trials (2B and 4B both answered "2" on 50/50 trials at every size). Choice mass was ~1.0, so this is
not a scoring failure.

The direct-association control (`$ZROOT/tmp/control_assoc_inf.py`, job 12915072) asks two questions on
the same episodes and same layout: the task's inference question (cue A → choose C, never studied) and
a **direct** one (cue A → choose B, a studied pair). Both fail identically — 2B and 4B direct accuracy
0.50–0.55, always answering "2". A model that scores 1.00 on recognition at n=500 cannot retrieve a
directly studied pair out of two chains, so the test turn is not being read.

**Hypothesis under test:** this test turn carries three images (cue + two options) where every working
2-AFC task carries two. Three layouts were added to `bench/tasks/associative_inference_eval.py`:

- `original` — "Cue image:" + cue, question, two options (one turn, three images).
- `cue_turn` — cue shown and acknowledged in its own turn, so the question turn holds exactly two
  images, the shape `afc_recognition`/`serial_order` use.
- `labeled` — "Image 1:"/"Image 2:" before each option, question last.

**The gate, fixed before the run:** a layout is usable only when *direct* retrieval rises above chance
with balanced answers. Inference accuracy means nothing until that holds.

**Result (job 12938460, complete): no layout passes the gate.** Direct A->B retrieval fails on both
models under all three layouts.

| Model | Layout | direct acc (n=2 / 5 / 25) | answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2B | original | 0.50 / 0.55 / 0.55 | "2" on 20/20 |
| 2B | cue_turn | 0.50 / 0.55 / 0.55 | "2" on 20/20 |
| 2B | labeled | 0.50 / 0.70 / 0.50 | split (12/20, 7/20, 3/20 chose "1") |
| 4B | original | 0.50 / 0.50 / 0.55 | "2" on 19-20/20 |
| 4B | cue_turn | 0.45 / 0.45 / 0.65 | "2" on 18-19/20 |
| 4B | labeled | 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.70 | split (5/20, 4/20, 5/20 chose "1") |

`labeled` does break the stuck bias — answers split instead of piling on "2" — so the models *are*
reading the options under it. Direct accuracy still does not clear chance: the two 0.70 cells are
14/20, which a binomial test does not separate from 0.5. So the three-image test turn was **not** the
cause, and fixing the turn is not sufficient.

**Next hypothesis: the study turn, and image-image binding specifically.** The AI study turn presents
image pairs separated only by a "Pair:" text marker, and the association to be retrieved is
image->image. Paired associates, which works (0.91-0.98 at n=1 on all three models), presents
image + "Word: X" and asks for image->word. The contrast suggests these models bind an image to text
but not an image to another image in this format. That is a reportable finding in its own right, not
just a broken task, but it needs one more control to stand: a direct-retrieval test holding everything
constant except whether the partner is an image or a word.

Do **not** rerun the sweep until a layout passes the gate; the 4B's sweep costs ~7 h. Three layouts
have now been tried and rejected.

The first sweep's 80 result files are archived at `results/archive_2026-09-26_assoc_inf_broken/`.
