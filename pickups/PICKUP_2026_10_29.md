### Completed (this session)

1. **Image corruption experiment built: the image analogue of the token pools (`clean` / `natural` / `scrambled`).**
   - User's brief: noise the images ImageNet-C style to test whether the image-vs-text results are about how in
     distribution the stimuli are. Two questions were separated on the way: *distribution* (same content, off-distribution
     pixels: ImageNet-C types) and *coherence* (images that are not of anything, as random-ID tokens are not).
   - `src/corruption.py`: 11 types at severities 1–5. ImageNet-C parameters (Hendrycks & Dietterich 2019) for
     gaussian_noise, shot_noise, impulse_noise, defocus_blur (FFT convolution: a direct one cost 70 ms/image), contrast,
     brightness, pixelate, jpeg_compression. Not ImageNet-C: patch_shuffle (c×c blocks permuted, c = 2…32),
     phase_scramble (random Fourier phase mixed in at weight 0.2…1.0, amplitude spectrum kept; DC phase zeroed, or the
     image mean flips to black), noise_image (uniform RGB noise in 32…1 px blocks, seeded by the original's pixels so each
     concept has its own noise image). Left out: glass/motion/zoom blur, snow, frost, fog, elastic (assets, ImageMagick,
     per-pixel loops). Every image is corrupted as a function of its pixels + run seed + severity, so the test copy equals
     the studied copy (otherwise recognition becomes a generalisation test). Applied before the evaluator's resize; the
     ImageNet crops are already 256 px. Cost: 1–17 ms per image; ~10 s per trial at n=2,500 on top of 28–58 s model time.
   - Config axis: a task lists `corruptions` and `severities`; image datasets gain `corruption`/`severity` in the condition
     (absent when unset, so the clean final runs are the severity-0 reference and keep their filenames); `tokens` refuses
     the keys. Wired in `bench/tasks/{afc_recognition,serial_order,pam}_eval.py` via `corruption.variants()` and
     `corruption.apply()` after `get_trials()`.
   - Tests `src/test_corruption.py` (run with the venv python; 10 checks pass): size/mode kept, distance monotone in
     severity (phase_scramble checked to weight 0.6 then "stays scrambled"), determinism per image, shuffle keeps the
     pixel multiset, amplitude spectrum kept, block noise, bad arguments, variants, apply on all three trial shapes,
     expansion + filenames, token refusal. Contact sheet `scripts/corruption_contact_sheet.py` →
     `plots/corruption_contact_sheet.png` (11 rows × clean + 5 severities, 3 crops).
   - Committed by Session 1 in 81afa49 (2026-10-27).
